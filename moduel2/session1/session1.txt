1. What is a Program?
A program is a set of instructions written in a programming language that tells a computer or mobile device what to do.
It helps perform specific tasks automatically and efficiently. For example, when I use Instagram, 
the program loads my feed, displays posts and reels, and lets me like, comment, and share content.

4. Algorithm for Ordering Food on Swiggy

1. Start.
2. Open the Swiggy app.
3. Log in to your account (if required).
4. Enter or confirm your delivery location.
5. Search for a restaurant or food item.
6. Select the restaurant.
7. Choose the food items.
8. Add the items to the cart.
9. Review the order.
10. Proceed to checkout.
11. Select the payment method.
12. Complete the payment.
13. Receive order confirmation.
14. Track the delivery.
15. End.
